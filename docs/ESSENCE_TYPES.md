# Essence Types

Veilborn essences describe the imbuement carried by a character. They replace the old elemental nature idea and are the future hook for transformation, lineage, and inherent powers.

## V1 Essences

- **Unimbued**: no animal essence; compatible with any lineage pairing.
- **Canine**: strength and agility leaning; future scent and pack abilities.
- **Serpent**: dexterity and intelligence leaning; future shedding and venom-sense abilities.
- **Ursine**: strength leaning; future resilience and grip abilities.
- **Feline**: agility leaning; future stealth and pounce abilities.

## Current Mechanics

- Essence values are stored on characters as `essence`.
- Pregnancy and lineage checks allow Unimbued with any essence.
- Two imbued characters are lineage-compatible only when their essences match.
- Essence abilities are catalog placeholders only; no combat powers are implemented yet.
